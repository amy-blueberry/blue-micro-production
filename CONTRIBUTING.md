# Contributing

## Evidence standards

- Label claims as observed, inferred, proposed or unknown.
- For numeric claims, record source, population, denominator, collection period and limitations.
- Keep platform users, video viewers, commenters, research participants and customers distinct.
- Treat public comments as qualitative observations. Never infer age, gender or location from profiles or language.
- Separate transcript analysis from audiovisual review and private analytics from public metadata.
- A proposed hypothesis stays untested until actual evidence has been collected. AI roleplay is not audience research.
- Record contrary findings and the observation that would change a decision.

## Research updates

1. Update the relevant evidence or hypothesis document with a supporting link.
2. Update the structured hypothesis register if its status changes.
3. Describe what changed, why, and the limits of the new evidence in the commit.
4. Check local document links and JSON syntax. Confirm that no confidential information or third-party media has been added unintentionally.

The register's statuses are UNTESTED, EXPLORING, SUPPORTED_FOR_SCOPE, MIXED and NOT_SUPPORTED. SUPPORTED_FOR_SCOPE must name the studied population and method; it does not mean universal validation.

## Participant and source handling

This repository is public. Keep recruitment contacts, consent records, participant-level identifiable data and nonpublic creator analytics in appropriately controlled systems. Publish only authorized, de-identified research summaries. Do not include raw transcripts, copied scripts, music, footage or internal business documents without the relevant authorization and rights review.

## Development gate

The current instruction is to research the audience and story possibilities before building episodes. Do not create scripts, episode assets or production work as a routine continuation of a research update. An explicit decision to resume development must establish the intended audience and remaining assumptions.
