## Micro-drama market findings

The production comparison distinguishes disclosed AI-generated releases, mixed catalogs, conventional filming and parent-level AI capability. Sources, dates and uncertainty sit beside the claims. These adult-market business benchmarks do not establish the audience or story preferences for Bumpp.

## Supplied creative and product context

Four [user-supplied documents](docs/bumpp-reference/README.md) are preserved as November 21, 2026 snapshots. Together they define a proposed 16–19 core design target, five audience cohorts, a story-development framework, ten scripted launch drops and a Roblox theater destination. Their strategic choices, reported scores and platform assertions retain their source status; this import is not an independent audience validation or authorization to produce episodes. The reference index connects them to the next company-analysis and agentic-loop pass.

## What we are trying to learn

1. Which viewing behaviors distinguish Roblox drama audiences from Roblox players generally?
2. Which age groups, viewing occasions and motivations should guide the creative brief?
3. Which relationships and story territories warrant development?
4. What evidence would change our assumptions before production begins?

The original “16-year-old girl in Los Angeles” was a supplied review persona, not a research finding. The working audience hypothesis is behavioral: viewers interested in serialized Roblox roleplay and character relationships. Age, gender, geography and active playing remain separate research questions.

## Workflow

Select an unresolved hypothesis → state supporting and disconfirming evidence → select a research method → collect actual observations → document scope and limitations → update the register → review the audience brief.

See the [validation playbook](docs/research/04-validation-playbook.md) for the decision gates. Passing a research gate does not authorize episode production; resuming development requires an explicit project decision.

## Repository boundaries

This repository contains research, references and a local intelligence application; it has no cloud-deployed production system or released episode. It contains original analysis, links to public sources, proposed procedures and the four explicitly authorized Bumpp documents, including supplied script drafts and a product spec. Their original labels are preserved. The separate unpublished internal-document collection, participant contact information and raw third-party transcripts are not included.

Source inspection date: November 21, 2026. Findings are date-bounded. Reference creators' production methods, audience demographics and causal success drivers must not be inferred from their popularity.
