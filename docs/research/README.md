# Bumpp: audience discovery before development

Research cut: 21 November 2026. Status: audience and story hypotheses; no new episodes, scripts, production assets or audience tests created.

The original 16-year-old girl in Los Angeles was a user-supplied review persona, not an independently discovered audience. The earlier 10–16 range was an editorial assumption. Neither should be used as a verified demographic baseline. This package supersedes those audience assumptions, not the historical record of the earlier draft.

The strongest working audience definition is behavioral: **English-language viewers who seek serialized Roblox roleplay stories and enjoy interpreting relationships, inhabiting a recurring social world and anticipating what happens next.** This is a research hypothesis, not a measured population description. Age, gender, geography, active playing and spending are separate questions.

Read in order:

1. [Audience evidence](01-audience-evidence.md): what public research establishes and what remains unknown.
2. [Reference mechanics](02-reference-mechanics.md): timestamped Walden analysis, contrasting shows and inspection limits.
3. [Story and format hypotheses](03-story-and-format-hypotheses.md): candidate relationship arcs and creative variables, without committing to a show.
4. [Validation playbook](04-validation-playbook.md): how to distinguish an appealing theory from audience evidence.
5. [Hypothesis register](05-hypothesis-register.json): structured status and decisions.
6. [Comment observation log](06-walden-comment-observations.json): bounded qualitative evidence, not demographic research.

Completed research: public market sources; full available auto-transcripts of Walden episodes 1 and 2, Strings Attached episode 1 and stinkella’s Royal Assistant episode 1; a bounded sample of 20 Top-sorted Walden episode 1 comments. These were transcript-based story analyses, not full audiovisual watches. Other supplied references have materially less coverage; see the inspection register.

Not completed: creator analytics access, representative audience survey, youth interviews, causal retention analysis, independent ranking of the recommended creators, or a complete viewing audit of every recommended show. No age or gender was inferred from usernames, avatars, wording or comments.

Internal Blueberry memo and onboarding document informed the earlier business and production brief. They do not establish demand for a particular story or viewer demographic. The original StoReel deck and seven screenshots remain unavailable in this workspace and contribute no verified figures here. Synced source files remain unchanged.

Decision now: investigate audience segments and compare story territories. Do not greenlight an episode from this document. The previous SECOND LOOK prototype is an unvalidated earlier creative experiment, not the selected concept.
