# Selection and ranking

User narrowed scope to the top 10 on November 21, 2026. Selection uses one comparable benchmark: Sensor Tower’s worldwide Q1 2026 short-drama app revenue ranking, reproduced on page 4 of [Adjust’s report](https://a.storyblok.com/f/47007/x/9a9afd9c10/short-drama-apps-report.pdf). Page layout was visually checked because extracted text reverses ranks 9 and 10.

1. DramaBox
2. ReelShort
3. NetShort
4. DramaWave
5. GoodShort
6. ShortMax
7. FlickReels
8. MyDrama
9. Stardust TV
10. FlareFlow

This is an app-level estimated IAP ranking, not a ranking of consolidated company revenue, profit, or advertising revenue. Parent/operators are mapped in every profile; shared ownership is not additive. It excludes web payments, advertising and third-party Android stores, and does not rank the full Chinese domestic ecosystem. Q1 2026 is a fixed comparable benchmark, not a claim about the November 2026 live leaderboard. Later company disclosures update the profiles without changing this selection.

Research completed before this scope change for smaller platforms is preserved outside the deliverable folder and excluded from the final archive. No extra company profiles will be added to the package.
