Every profile follows the six requested sections: ownership; strategy; monetization; traction; titles/IP; moat and risks. Citations sit beside the claims they support. [Sources](SOURCES.md) provides a grouped index.

Evidence labels matter: regulatory filings describe their named legal entities; app stores describe a region and observation date; analytics vendors provide estimates; companies and partners provide attributed claims. Analyst interpretations are identified explicitly. A disclosed AI product or AI-assisted localization process does not establish fully autonomous end-to-end production. Public sources generally do not disclose reliable app-specific CAC/LTV, exact channel mix, or title-level receipts; those limits are retained rather than filled with invented figures.

[QUALITY_REPORT.md](QUALITY_REPORT.md) records the four checks and follow-up count. A PASS means the requested evidence-completeness gate was met at the entity level stated in the profile. It does not certify audited financials or a complete legal ownership register. [PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md) describes the sequential research and review process; `queue.json`, `evidence/`, `judgments/` and `events.jsonl` retain the review state.

This directory contains the ten selected profiles and supporting public-source research. Internal business documents and third-party media are not part of this research update. Earlier broad discovery is outside this scope.
