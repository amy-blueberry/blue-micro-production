## Material qualifications

- Ownership/HQ is assessed at the explicitly identified operator, brand or parent level. GoodShort's holding chain remains reported and its brand HQ differs from its legal operator address. ReelShort's COL investment is non-controlling; it is not labeled a wholly owned COL business. DramaBox's later holding-company transaction is retained.
- Quantitative coverage does not require two revenue figures. Store install thresholds are cumulative Android lower bounds, not MAU. Company download totals and store counts overlap. Funding belongs to the named parent, not automatically the app.
- Reporting dates are preserved when a source does not disclose an exact measurement month, notably ShortMax's peak MAU. Such a figure supports dated reported scale, not a monthly time series.
- FlickReels' extreme growth estimate combines IAP and advertising and uses a small launch-period baseline; it is not an absolute revenue estimate or directly comparable with the IAP ranking.
- FlareFlow's subsidiary accounts, DramaWave's billings and vendor app IAP estimates have different accounting scopes. Combined DramaWave/FreeReels billings are supplementary and must not be assigned entirely to DramaWave.
- Featured or company-described successful titles are not represented as verified highest-grossing titles. Production plans, catalog stocks, localization throughput and delivered originals are distinguished.

FlickReels required one targeted ownership/HQ follow-up. First-party Zhangwan product and recruitment pages resolved the group attribution and Hangzhou headquarters. No entity was marked complete through the two-retry exception rule.
