# ReelShort

Research date: 2026-11-21. Entity level: App operated by Crazy Maple Studio; distinguish app spending from parent-company accounts.

For the later cross-company production-method comparison and additional title-level evidence, see [AI versus conventional production](../AI_VS_TRADITIONAL.md).

## Company & Ownership

ReelShort is operated by Crazy Maple Studio (CMS), whose company profile identifies Sunnyvale, California as headquarters. Apple lists NewLeaf Publishing as the store developer. CMS was founded in 2016; ReelShort launched in 2022. COL’s H1 2026 filing identifies CMS as an equity-accounted associate with a 49.24% interest, not a wholly owned consolidated subsidiary. A complete current capitalization table and latest funding valuation are not established by the reviewed disclosures. [Company profile](https://www.linkedin.com/company/crazymaplestudio), [CMS statement](https://crazymaplestudios.com/statement), [COL filing, printed p.157](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-26/1225504423.PDF), [launch history](https://time.com/6979994/crazy-maple-studio/).

## Core Strategy & Positioning

Mobile-first vertical serials with a US-facing foundation and international distribution. The 2024 CEO interview describes female-focused romance and high drama, in-house development, and revising stories using payment and drop-off evidence. These are dated executive statements, not proof of a current fully AI-generated pipeline. SocialPeta’s 2025 advertising review documents a broad creative-acquisition operation and genre expansion. [CEO interview](https://time.com/7173765/reelshort-crazy-maple-studio-joey-jia-interview/), [SocialPeta report](https://sp2cdn-idea-global.zingfront.com/report-preview/2026/SocialPeta-x-Amo-Pictures-%7C-Insight-into-Global-Micro-Drama-App-Marketing-for-2026.pdf).

## Monetization & Unit Economics

Free download, paid episode access and renewable subscriptions. The US Apple listing inspected November 21, 2026 describes a seven-day subscription and lists a $19.99 subscription SKU plus one-time purchases, including $4.99 and $9.99. Storefront prices and offers can vary; the listing does not establish a universal coin-to-episode exchange rate. The reviewed disclosures do not provide a reconciled advertising/IAP/direct-web revenue split or cohort LTV/CAC. Do not treat estimated consumer spending as net revenue or contribution margin. [US store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/reelshort-stream-drama-tv/id1636235979).

## Competitive Moat & Risks

Analyst interpretation: accumulated story-development knowledge, a recognizable app brand and an established paid-acquisition operation can reinforce one another. Risks include high acquisition dependence, substitutable tropes, subscription friction and inaccurate ownership/accounting comparisons. The evidence does not disclose defensible cohort payback periods; scale alone does not establish profitability.
