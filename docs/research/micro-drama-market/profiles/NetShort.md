# NetShort

Research date: 2026-11-21. Entity level: App owned by Maiya; Singapore store operator.

For the later cross-company production-method comparison and additional title-level evidence, see [AI versus conventional production](../AI_VS_TRADITIONAL.md).

## Company & Ownership

NetShort is the overseas platform of Chongqing Maiya Media, whose headquarters are in Chongqing, China. NETSTORY PTE. LTD., Singapore, is the app seller. Sources place launch in 2024 but disagree on month: SocialPeta says July; BidMachine says October. This may reflect release versus commercial rollout, but the distinction is not established. No completed external financing round is verified here. [Maiya HQ](https://www.maiyawx.cn/news), [ownership](https://www.cqnews.net/web/content_1572223207679287296.html), [seller](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.netshort.abroad), [launch reports](https://sp2cdn-idea-global.zingfront.com/report-preview/2025/Insight-into-Global-Micro-Drama-App-Marketing-for-H1-2025.pdf), [partner case](https://www.bidmachine.com/blog/how-netshort-grew-ad-revenue).

## Core Strategy & Positioning

Translated Chinese series supplied early expansion; local productions and AI animation broaden the catalog. A November 17, 2026 partnership opens Maiya’s AI creation tools, training and distribution to creators. The Trade Desk documents connected-TV acquisition through MadHouse, using cross-device measurement and shifting spend from weaker audience/inventory combinations to stronger ones. These are specific partner-reported processes, not access to NetShort’s internal stack. [Creator pipeline](https://www.cqnews.net/web/content_1572223207679287296.html), [CTV case](https://www.thetradedesk.com/case-studies/netshort-drives-growth-with-full-funnel-ctv-user-acquisition-strategy).

## Monetization & Unit Economics

Coins, weekly VIP and advertising coexist. US iOS snapshot November 21, 2026 lists weekly VIP offers at $5.99/$19.99/$24.99/$29.99; eligibility differs and is not specified. BidMachine’s April 16, 2026 case claims its own ad-revenue contribution doubled and app ARPU rose over 5%; this is not a doubling of total company revenue. Absolute CAC/LTV and a total ad/IAP split remain undisclosed. [Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/netshort-popular-dramas-tv/id6504849169), [monetization partner](https://www.bidmachine.com/blog/how-netshort-grew-ad-revenue).

## Competitive Moat & Risks

Analyst interpretation: domestic studio experience, multilingual distribution and ad-monetization optimization can reinforce each other. Risks include marketing dependence and misattributing a partner-specific uplift to the whole company. Store age labels and adult tropes are not evidence that the audience resembles Roblox teens.
