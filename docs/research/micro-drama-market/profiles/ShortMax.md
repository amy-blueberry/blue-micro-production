# ShortMax

Research date: 2026-11-21. Entity level: App; Jiuzhou parent and AI joint venture kept separate.

For the later cross-company production-method comparison and additional title-level evidence, see [AI versus conventional production](../AI_VS_TRADITIONAL.md).

## Company & Ownership

ShortMax is Jiuzhou Culture’s international platform. The service terms and Apple seller identify ShortTV Limited. Jiuzhou’s own contact page explicitly gives its headquarters in Jiaxing, Zhejiang, China. The parent says the app launched in 2023; September is reported by industry coverage. In October 2026 NetDragon announced an AI Harness joint venture with Jiuzhou, not an acquisition of ShortMax itself. Standalone app equity funding and a complete shareholder register are not disclosed by these sources. [Parent](https://www.jxjzwh.cn/business-abroad.html), [HQ](https://www.jxjzwh.cn/join.html), [terms](https://www.shorttv.live/upload), [JV announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/netdragon-partners-with-jiuzhou-culture-to-establish-joint-venture-302913397.html).

## Core Strategy & Positioning

North America is the stated strategic core, with Europe and additional international markets served through translated Chinese dramas and locally produced originals. Jiuzhou describes coordination between production bases and local teams. This is a localization-and-production model, rather than a claim that one Chinese catalog is merely subtitled everywhere. SocialPeta documents substantial creative advertising activity, but the reviewed material does not quantify net acquisition spend. [Parent strategy](https://www.jxjzwh.cn/business-abroad.html), [2024 advertising review](https://sp2cdn-idea-global.zingfront.com/report-preview/2025/SocialPeta-X-Smadex-_-2024-Global-Mobile-Apps-Marketing-Trends-White-Paper.pdf).

## Monetization & Unit Economics

Free download with paid access; the US Apple listing includes Weekly Pass Pro at $19.99 and one-time purchase options. Snapshot: November 21, 2026. Exact coin amounts, episode prices, offer eligibility and ad unlock rules should be tested by market before modeling a full-series cost. The sources do not provide an audited advertising/IAP split, cohort retention, CAC or LTV. A separate AI joint-venture investment is not app revenue. [US storefront](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shortmax-short-dramas-tv/id6464002625).

This establishes a promoted title and its body-image/romance positioning, not audited title profitability. Jiuzhou’s wider rights library includes both distribution rights and productions; neither the entire library nor parent output should be called ShortMax originals. A verified weekly original-release cadence is not supplied by the reviewed sources. ## Competitive Moat & Risks

Analyst interpretation: access to a production organization and regional localization can support faster catalog replenishment. Risks include paid acquisition dependence, competition from free offerings and inconsistent public definitions of registered users, downloads and MAU. The newly announced AI infrastructure JV may change production tooling; its completion and commercial results cannot be assumed from an MOU.

Evidence gate: **PASS**. Parent, service operator and parent HQ are identified; two dated reported scale figures are present with peak-period limitations. No claim of independent financial audit or closed app funding is made.
