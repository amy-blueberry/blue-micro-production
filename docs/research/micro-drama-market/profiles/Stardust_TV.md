# Stardust TV

Research date: 2026-11-21. Entity level: App / Shanhai overseas brand; Hong Kong legal operator.

For the later cross-company production-method comparison and additional title-level evidence, see [AI versus conventional production](../AI_VS_TRADITIONAL.md).

## Company & Ownership

StardustTV is Shanhai Group’s overseas platform. The parent, Guangxi Shanhai Xingchen Media Group, identifies Nanning, Guangxi, China as headquarters. Hongxing Media Co., Limited is the Hong Kong service operator and store seller. CRI reports launch on June 30, 2024; store trackers place iOS availability in early July. No separately verified financing round is disclosed in the reviewed sources. [Parent HQ](https://m.gxshxc.com/), [parent/app relationship](https://pandashorts.cri.cn/20260115/51d448c4-d1d1-4cae-a27f-4c0fc6a292de.html), [operator privacy policy](https://www.stardusttv.net/de/privacy-agreement), [store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stardusttv-short-drama-movie/id6502377927).

## Core Strategy & Positioning

Early export reused the parent’s Chinese drama rights, followed by localized originals. CRI describes AI translation with human review, in-house localization, performance-based creative placement and a content-management system. The privacy policy independently names EngageLab Push for notifications and TurboLink for attribution/deep links; disclosure confirms declared SDK use, not measured causal retention improvement. [Production reporting](https://pandashorts.cri.cn/20260115/51d448c4-d1d1-4cae-a27f-4c0fc6a292de.html), [SDK disclosures](https://www.stardusttv.net/de/privacy-agreement).

## Monetization & Unit Economics

US iOS snapshot November 21, 2026: weekly subscriptions $19.99/$29.99; 500 coins $4.99 and 2,000 coins $19.99. Other subscription offers have less specific labels, so their duration is not inferred. Apple declares advertising. A verified ad/IAP split, payer LTV or CAC is not published in these sources. [Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stardusttv-short-drama-movie/id6502377927).

## Traction & Financial Data Points

• November 21, 2026 observation: **10m+ cumulative Android downloads**, a Google Play lower-bound bucket. [Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.shdj.test).
• By June 2025: reported localization throughput **over 1,000 episodes per month**, described in CRI’s January 15, 2026 feature. This is translated output, not newly filmed episodes. [Workflow reporting](https://pandashorts.cri.cn/20260115/51d448c4-d1d1-4cae-a27f-4c0fc6a292de.html).
The same feature reports 30m+ downloads and 10m+ MAU reached within roughly a year of launch, but gives no exact measurement month; those figures are not used for the strict dated-metric gate.

## Competitive Moat & Risks

Analyst interpretation: domestic rights inventory, in-house localization and behavioral messaging can support a repeatable export operation. Risks include cultural mismatch, subscription friction and treating translation capacity as original-production capacity. Marketing claims about rapid turnaround are not substitutes for end-to-end delivery logs or costed production accounts.

Evidence gate: **PASS**. Reported parent/app relationship, first-party HQ and legal operator verified. Dated downloads and translation output pass; imprecisely dated MAU excluded from gate.
