# FlickReels

Research date: 2026-11-21. Entity level: App and disclosed parent/operator; intermediate legal shareholding chain not established.

For the later cross-company production-method comparison and additional title-level evidence, see [AI versus conventional production](../AI_VS_TRADITIONAL.md).

## Company & Ownership

Zhangwan Technology identifies FlickReels as its overseas app; its corporate recruitment page explicitly identifies headquarters in Hangzhou, Zhejiang. FARSUN PTE. LTD. is the Singapore app-store operator. This corroborates group attribution, without establishing every intermediate legal shareholding entity. The app launched in 2024, with July specified by SocialPeta. No completed financing round is verified in these materials. [Parent product page](https://zhangwan.com/business/), [Headquarters](https://www.zhangwan.com/join/), [Store operator](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.zyhwplatform.shortplay), [Launch chronology](https://sp2cdn-idea-global.zingfront.com/report-preview/2025/Insight-into-Global-Micro-Drama-App-Marketing-for-H1-2025.pdf).

## Core Strategy & Positioning

SocialPeta identifies older-character romance and period dramas as early differentiators, alongside Southeast Asian expansion. Current storefronts broaden the offering to CEOs, werewolves, revenge and fantasy. Official links connect TikTok, YouTube and Facebook to the app. iOS markets AI co-creation, whereas the inspected Android copy uses broader storytelling language; neither demonstrates an autonomous production pipeline or measured recommendation uplift. [Historical segment strategy](https://sp2cdn-idea-global.zingfront.com/report-preview/2025/Insight-into-Global-Micro-Drama-App-Marketing-for-H1-2025.pdf), [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flickreels-ai-story-drama/id6514312894), [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.zyhwplatform.shortplay). Zhangwan explicitly describes Facebook and TikTok promotion into Europe, North America and Southeast Asia. [Parent distribution statement](https://zhangwan.com/business/).

## Monetization & Unit Economics

US iOS snapshot November 21, 2026: weekly membership $12.99/$19.99 and monthly membership $39.99; one-time purchase SKUs include $4.99/$9.99/$19.99 without coin quantities. Advertising is declared. A developer reply acknowledges rewarded episode unlocking, but a company-wide ad/IAP split, CAC and LTV are not published. [Apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flickreels-ai-story-drama/id6514312894), [Google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.zyhwplatform.shortplay).
