# DramaWave

Research date: 2026-11-21. Entity level: Paid app within Kunlun short-drama portfolio.

For the later cross-company production-method comparison and additional title-level evidence, see [AI versus conventional production](../AI_VS_TRADITIONAL.md).

## Company & Ownership

DramaWave is Kunlun Tech’s paid overseas short-drama platform, operated by Singapore-based SKYWORK AI PTE. LTD. Kunlun is headquartered in Beijing and listed in Shenzhen; headquarters and listing venue should not be confused. The app entered international markets in September 2024. Parent backing is established, but a separate DramaWave venture round is not. [Parent filing](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-26/1223323720.PDF), [HQ](https://cn.linkedin.com/company/kunlun-inc), [launch](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/State-of-Short-Drama-Apps-Overseas-2025-Report.pdf), [operator](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.dramawave.app).

## Core Strategy & Positioning

Kunlun’s Q3 2026 filing distinguishes DramaWave’s paid, higher-ARPU focus in Europe/North America/Japan/Korea from FreeReels’ ad-supported emerging-market strategy. It emphasizes suspense/adventure, urban relationships and fantasy/action. SkyProduction is described as supporting scripts, assets, storyboards, generated video, post-production and quality checks. Social promotion is documented by Sensor Tower; no internal experiment threshold is disclosed. [Q3 filing p.9](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-10-30/1225644210.PDF), [distribution analysis](https://cdn.asiaecs.com/static/upload/file/common/2025/05/26/1748230211799671.pdf).

## Monetization & Unit Economics

US iOS listing on November 21, 2026: weekly VIP $19.99, monthly VIP $36.99, plus purchase offers such as $6.99/$9.99/$19.99. Advertising is declared. Coin/unlock terms and promotional eligibility vary; the SKU table alone does not establish full-series cost. A reconciled app-only ad/IAP split and cohort CAC/LTV are not supplied. [Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dramawave-dramas-reels/id6670430706).

## Traction & Financial Data Points

• March 2025: approximately **$10m monthly gross billings**, with roughly **$120m annualized** billings reported by Kunlun. Annualization is not realized annual revenue. [2024 annual-report summary, published April 2025](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-26/1223323720.PDF).
• November 21, 2026: **100m+ cumulative Android downloads**, store bucket. [Google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.dramawave.app).
• September 2026: nearly **$100m monthly gross billings for Kunlun’s combined AI-drama platforms**, including FreeReels; explicitly not DramaWave-only revenue. [Q3 filing](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-10-30/1225644210.PDF).

## Notable Titles & IP

Kunlun claims **over 95% of September 2026 new releases were AI-generated**. That is the share of one month’s additions, not of the entire library or proof of fully autonomous production. *Race to My Heart* is documented as an April 2025 DramaWave-channel promotion; title-level receipts are not published. A stable completed-series-per-month cadence is not established. [Q3 filing](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-10-30/1225644210.PDF), [promotion record](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/%40dramawaveapp/).

## Competitive Moat & Risks

Analyst interpretation: shared creation tools and a paid/free distribution portfolio can lower genre-testing and localization costs. Risks include AI quality/rights control, marketing expenses and misleading comparisons between gross billings, annualized run rates and recognized revenue. The parent’s multi-app figures must not be counted once for every app.

Evidence gate: **PASS**. Parent/HQ and operator corroborated; dated app billings and downloads pass. Annualized and combined-platform metrics cannot be substituted for standalone realized revenue.
