# AI-generated versus conventional production

Updated November 21, 2026. Covers the same ten selected apps; this is an evidence classification, not a new revenue ranking or a census of every title.

**The market is mixed.** Some companies distribute both filmed performances and generated animation/video. AI use in writing, recommendations, advertising, dubbing or editing alone does not make the finished drama AI-generated. Conversely, an AI-generated drama can still have human writers, directors and editors.

## How the production methods differ

| Stage | Conventional filming | Generated-video production | Mixed production |
|---|---|---|---|
| Development | Writers develop premise, characters and script | Writers/directors may use models to explore and draft | Same creative decisions, with AI used selectively |
| Performance and footage | Cast, locations/sets, camera and sound crews | Character references, generated shots, voice assets, human selection and revision | Filmed performances combined with generated sequences or effects |
| Post-production | Editing, sound, color, VFX and localization | Editing, continuity fixes, regeneration, voice/lip-sync and compositing | Conventional editing plus selected AI operations |
| Evidence needed | Cast/crew credits, behind-the-scenes records | Title disclosures, generation workflow, shot provenance | Stage-by-stage records identifying what was filmed or generated |

This workflow table describes the methods; it is not a claim that every company uses every listed step. Conventional production can use AI tools without replacing its filmed performances. Roblox machinima is a separate footage method: capture/animation inside a game engine does not itself imply generative AI.

## What this changes in the research

ReelShort and Stardust TV should not be classified as conventional-only from their older operating histories. DramaBox also has named supplier-disclosed generated series. None of these findings establishes an unattended ideation-to-publication system, a fully AI-produced catalog, or that AI caused an app's revenue rank.

For further production-stack research, prioritize DramaWave and FlareFlow for explicit platform-level disclosures; use DramaBox's supplier case as a concrete human-directed generation example. My Muse is relevant within HOLYWATER, but is not an eleventh ranked app in this package. These are analyst priorities, not evidence that the content fits a Roblox youth audience.
