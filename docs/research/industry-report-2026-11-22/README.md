# Micro-drama industry report for Claude Design

Research cut: **November 22, 2026**. Prepared for Blueberry / Bumpp.

## Share these files with Claude Design

1. [INDUSTRY_REPORT.md](INDUSTRY_REPORT.md) — the complete research narrative, ten company profiles, AI comparison, workflows, economics, audience analysis, risks and Bumpp implications.
2. [CLAUDE_DESIGN_BRIEF.md](CLAUDE_DESIGN_BRIEF.md) — a ready-to-use request, 36-slide narrative, 10 appendix topics, design direction and numerical guardrails.
3. [chart_data.json](chart_data.json) — 16 chart/table datasets with dates, units, source IDs, qualifiers and limitations; includes a reproducible synthetic economics example.
4. [SOURCE_REGISTER.md](SOURCE_REGISTER.md) and [sources.json](sources.json) — readable and structured source registers.

The main report is self-contained. The original company profiles remain in the repository's separate micro-drama market package. This handoff contains original analysis and links, not copied source PDFs, third-party footage or the unpublished internal Blueberry collection.

## Scope and safeguards

- Ten apps selected by **Q1 2026 worldwide estimated IAP revenue rank**, not ten highest-revenue consolidated companies or a live November ranking.
- Company filings, vendor estimates, marketing claims, analyst interpretation and proposed workflows retain separate labels.
- Market data does not establish the age/gender composition of Roblox drama viewers.
- Company claims are attributed; structural validation is not an audit of private accounts.
- Missing full StoReel deck and earlier screenshots contributed no verified statistics.
- The synthetic unit-economics example is a teaching model, not a company benchmark or operating forecast.
- Episode development remains paused; the supplied scripts were not expanded or regenerated.

## Verification

`validate_report.py` checks structured data, source IDs, fixed ranks, numerical consistency, the synthetic model and local document links. It emits [VALIDATION.json](VALIDATION.json). [manifest.json](manifest.json) records file hashes. These checks detect artifact inconsistencies; they do not independently verify every external source claim.
