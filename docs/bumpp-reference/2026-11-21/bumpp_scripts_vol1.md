## Are these the ten I'd bet on?

**Honest answer about the previous slate: no, not all ten.** Four of the earlier shows were clever originals without a proven spine or with a single point of failure: *Carry* (a sports drama for the cohort least likely to watch drama), *Still Here* (a niche, quiet nostalgia story), *B-Side* (proven idol format, but the whole show depends on one original song testing well), and *Night Shift* (strong, but a rules-horror format with less direct evidence than the alternatives). They're benched, not deleted.

**They were replaced with the most proven spines in the category, executed with taste:**

- ***Swapped***: the switched-at-birth true heiress (*True Heiress vs. Fake Queen Bee* passed 395M views on ReelShort).
- ***Full Ride***: enemies to lovers across a class divide at an elite academy (the most-cited BookTok trope; *Walden Academy*'s premise).
- ***Rejected***: the rejected fated mate who returns transformed (one of ReelShort's foundational genres and its own serial-fiction shelf).
- ***Last One Online***: the elimination game (elimination games already have hundreds of millions of Roblox visits).

**And one kept show was fixed:** *Default* now delivers the glow-up. The first version refused the transformation, which denied the payoff its arc exists for.

## How to read this volume

- **Every show is vertical-first.** Each script is Chapters 1 to 10, the launch drop, followed by daily chapters. Long-form episodes are compilations cut from chapters.
- **Each chapter is written on the clock**, with bold timestamps for every beat: HOOK, RECAP-RESOLVE, FRICTION, SPIKE, TURN, BUTTON.
- **Each cover page** shows who it's for, why, the summaries, the four house ingredients, the proven arc spine and its obligatory beats, the Bankability Score, key choices, what triggers each viewer action, gate scores, what every pass changed, and the full audit report.

**Recommended production order:** *Swapped*, *Full Ride*, *Rejected* first (the highest-confidence spines, all Drama Girls, the largest drama audience), then *Default* and *Plus One*. Launch *Maple Court* alongside them as the brand's taste flagship, not as the first bet. Hook-test every show before production: three 15-second cold opens each, posted as organic clips (V10.3, K7).

**What "would be successful" can and can't mean.** These ten are the slate I'd bet on: proven spines, proven demand, hit beat-for-beat, written to the microdrama clock, with our taste and voice. No script can guarantee a hit. Execution (voice acting, animation, faces at the button, thumbnails) and real audience data decide the rest. The checkers prove the structure is there; the hook test and retention data prove it works.

**Ages:** romance-led leads are 18+; *Unfollowed* (17), the 2020 timeline of *Maple Court* (17), and Wren in *Rejected* (17) stay at loyalty, longing, and family.
